THE WORKSHOP

Inside the Verse.

A club inauguration meets a cloud buildathon. Discover the tools, meet the community, and take your first project live.

Explore the cloud

Find your way around the AWS Console, play service bingo, and hear how alumni made the jump from campus to Amazon.

Build a student portal

Launch an EC2 server, deploy a web app, and connect it to an RDS database. Test it by adding and retrieving student records.

Take the challenge

Team up to design an AWS architecture, explain your choices, and put your cloud knowledge to the test in the final quiz.

BEFORE YOU SWING IN

Field guide.

Who is this workshop for?

Students who are curious about cloud computing. We start with AWS fundamentals and build up to a working application over two days.

What should I bring?

Bring your laptop and charger. Follow the organizers’ lab-access and student verification instructions before you arrive.

How will the labs work?

Work in teams of around four to five with an assigned AWS lab environment. Share the server, database, deployment, and testing roles.

When and where is the workshop?

November 27 and 28, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM each day. Both days are in person. The venue is ENL Block Seminar Hall, Ground Floor, BVRIT Narsapur.
